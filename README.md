# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms cleaned e-commerce sales and customer data into interactive visual insights covering **revenue, profit, orders, customers, customer segments, payment methods, sales channels, and regional performance**.

The goal of this project is to understand e-commerce business performance and provide a clear view of sales and customer trends through data visualization.

---

##  Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – KPI and analytical calculations
* **CSV** – Source data
* **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts and KPI cards

##  Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis

### 2. Customer Segment Analysis

* Consumer
* Premium
* VIP
* Business

### 3. Sales Channel Analysis

Analyzes sales across different channels such as:

* Mobile App
* Website
* Marketplace
* Social Media

### 4. Customer Segment Sales

Compares total net sales across different customer segments.

### 5. Payment Method Analysis

Analyzes sales generated through:

* Credit Card
* Debit Card
* PayPal
* Digital Wallet
* Cash on Delivery
* Buy Now Pay Later
